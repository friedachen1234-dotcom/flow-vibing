Harissa chicken with charred lemons

Saved to your cookbook
35 minTotal
10 minPrep
EasyDifficulty
520kcal / serving

Ingredients Serves 4

  • Chicken thighs, bone in8
  • Rose harissa3 tbsp
  • Lemons2
  • Red onions2
  • Baby potatoeshalved600 g
  • Garlic cloves4
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • Greek yoghurtto serve150 g
  • Flat-leaf parsleya handful

Method

Everything goes on one tray. The lemons char into something sweet and sticky while the harissa turns the pan juices into a sauce you'll want bread for.

Heat the oven to 220°C fan. Toss the potatoes and onions with half the oil on a large roasting tray.
Rub the chicken with harissa, the rest of the oil, and plenty of salt. Nestle among the vegetables, skin side up.
Halve the lemons, squeeze one over the tray, and tuck all four halves cut side down between the chicken.25 min roast
Roast until the skin is deep red and blistered and the potatoes are tender.
Rest for five minutes, then spoon over the pan juices and finish with yoghurt and parsley.
